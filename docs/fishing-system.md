# Fishing System — Deep & Extremely Fun

## Design Goal
Fishing should be a major parallel progression path that feels as rewarding as combat. Players should be able to spend hours just fishing and still make meaningful progress.

**Hard requirement: Minimum 300 unique fish.**

---

## Fish Design

### Rarity Tiers (same language as existing loot)
- Common
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary
- Mythic / Cosmic

### Fish Types (examples of variety)
- Basic river fish
- Lake dwellers
- Saltwater / ocean
- Deep sea abyssal
- Bioluminescent
- Elemental (fire, frost, storm, poison)
- Ghost / spectral
- Cosmic / void
- Living metal
- Plant-fish hybrids
- Tiny "jewelry" fish that sell for high gold
- Massive boss-sized fish that fight back

Every fish has:
- Unique 3D model + animations
- Sell price range
- Optional special drop (scales, oils, eyes, etc. used in crafting)
- Biome + water type preferences
- Time-of-day and weather preferences
- Size variance (affects sell price and some recipes)

**Target: 300+ distinct fish** with different looks, sizes, behaviors, and economic value.

---

## Fishing Rods

Rods are full gear pieces with:
- Base Power
- Base Luck
- Cast Distance / Accuracy
- Line Strength
- Special Effects / Enchantments

### Rod Examples
- Basic Wooden Rod
- Reinforced Oak
- Steelspine
- Enchanted Willow
- Abyssal Deeprod
- Cosmic Angler
- Legendary named rods with unique mechanics

Rods support the full socket + charm + special perk system (see combat-weapons doc).

### Special Rod Effects (examples)
- Increased chance of rare fish
- Auto-reel on small fish
- Chance to catch two fish at once
- Lure that attracts specific families
- "Lucky Strike" — next catch has massively boosted rarity
- Elemental attraction (fire rods pull fire fish harder)

---

## Fishing Locations
Every biome has multiple named fishing spots:
- Ponds
- Lakes
- Rivers
- Waterfalls
- Underground pools
- Coastal shallows
- Deep ocean platforms
- Floating sky lakes
- Magical fountains

Some spots are only accessible by boat or after building a dock.

---

## Fishing Gameplay Loop
1. Equip rod + optional bait / lure
2. Cast (skill shot or auto-aim with better rods)
3. Wait / mini-game when a fish bites (tension, direction, stamina)
4. Land the fish
5. Decide: sell, cook, craft, or keep for collection / aquarium

Higher skill + better rod + pet bonuses + time/weather = dramatically better results.

---

## Economy Link
Fish sell for gold. High-end fish sell for *a lot*.  
Rare fish parts are used in high-end crafting, potions, and pet food.  
This makes fishing a legitimate way to fund builds and gear.
