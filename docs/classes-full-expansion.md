# Full Class Expansion — Going Way Further

Original 5 stay. I’m now proposing a much richer class roster and deeper identity for each.

---

## Original Classes — Deepened

### Crusader → Oathbound
Still holy warrior, but now has three Oath paths players choose between:
- Oath of the Shield (pure tank + group protection)
- Oath of the Flame (aggressive holy fire)
- Oath of the Martyr (self-sacrifice for massive power)

### Necromancer → Grave Sovereign
Instead of just “summon skeletons”, the class now builds a personal **Undead Court**.  
You raise specific named undead that level with you and have personalities.  
You can also temporarily become a full lich form.

### Sorcerer → Elemental Sovereign
Players eventually attune to one primary element so hard that the world reacts to them (rain follows a storm sorcerer, plants wither near a fire one, etc.).

### Ranger → Wildmark
Gains a permanent animal companion that is stronger than normal pets and can be swapped between several bonded animals. Strong synergy with the hunting system.

### Monk → Ascendant
At high level can enter short “Ascension” states where they temporarily become pure energy / spirit form.

## New Classes (Full Proposals)

### 1. Beastcaller (Highest Priority)
The morph class you asked for, fully realized.

**Core Fantasy:** You don’t just fight animals — you *become* them.

- Hunt or bond with animals → unlock their morph
- Morph has its own skill bar, movement, and resource
- Mastery system: the more you use a form, the stronger and longer it becomes
- Ultimate: Chimera Form — temporary hybrid of 2–3 animals at once

Sub-paths:
- Apex Predator (combat morphs)
- Spirit Walker (mystical / elemental animals)
- Pack Leader (summon animal allies while morphed)

### 2. Artificer
Gunsmith + gadgeteer + vehicle specialist.

- Can deploy turrets, drones, and mobile workshops
- Strongest class with firearms and vehicles
- Can temporarily overclock any vehicle or weapon for massive power at a risk

### 3. Warden
Nature fortress builder.

- Can grow living walls and structures
- Commands plants and lesser animals
- Ultimate turns a large area into a hostile garden that fights for them

### 4. Corsair
Naval / coastal specialist.

- Massive bonuses on boats and in water
- Can board enemy ships or pull players into naval duels
- Best fishing synergy of any class
- Can summon temporary ghost ships

### 5. Voidwalker (Space / Late Game Class)
Unlocked after reaching the space layer.

- Teleportation and gravity manipulation
- Can open small rifts
- Extremely mobile and slippery
- Unique space combat tools

### 6. Echo Knight
Fights with afterimages and time echoes of themselves.

- Can leave “echoes” that repeat your last few actions
- Strong duelist fantasy
- Can temporarily fight as two or three versions of yourself

### 7. Plague Doctor / Alchemist
Throws vials, creates zones, mutates enemies and themselves.

- Can temporarily mutate into stronger (but unstable) forms
- Strong synergy with hunting (uses animal parts in concoctions)

## Class Fantasy Goals

Every class should answer:
1. What does this class do that no one else can?
2. What does the world look like when this class walks through it?
3. What is the “holy shit” moment at high level?

I want each class to feel like its own small fantasy game inside the bigger game.
