# Spaceship Construction, Space Travel & Infinite Planets

This is now a core pillar of the game.

Players will gather materials across the main world (and later from other planets) to build, upgrade, and pilot their own spaceship. From there they can leave the main planet and explore an effectively infinite number of procedurally generated planets.

---

## 1. Material Gathering & Tools (New Core Loop)

### New Tool Family
Players need proper tools to harvest the world efficiently:

| Tool | Primary Use | Progression |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Axe | Chop trees / wood | Basic → Steel → Crystal → Voidforged |
| Pickaxe | Mine stone, ore, crystals | Same tiers |
| Shovel | Dig soft materials, roots, sand | — |
| Sickle / Scythe | Harvest plants, fibers, special flora | — |
| Hammer | Break certain structures / refined materials | — |
| Multitool (late) | Combines several functions | High-end |

Tools have durability, sockets, charms, and the same Special Perk system as weapons. A god-roll pickaxe can be more valuable than most weapons.

### Resource Nodes Across the Main World
- Wood (multiple types)
- Stone / Rock
- Basic ores (iron, copper, etc.)
- Rare crystals
- Organic materials (vines, resins, bones)
- Special biome-only materials

Higher-tier tools let you harvest higher-tier nodes and get better yields.

---

## 2. Spaceship Construction

### Core Fantasy
Players don’t just “unlock a ship”. They **build** it piece by piece from materials they gathered.

### Construction Stages
1. **Frame / Hull** — basic structure
2. **Engine** — determines speed and fuel efficiency
3. **Life Support** — how long you can stay in space
4. **Cargo Hold** — storage capacity
5. **Weapons / Defense** (optional but important)
6. **Landing Gear & Exploration Modules**
7. **Advanced Systems** (teleporters, scanners, living quarters, pet habitats, aquariums, etc.)

Players can build the ship in a large open area or at special shipyards that appear later.

### Ship Customization
- Visual style (sleek, industrial, organic, cosmic, pirate, etc.)
- Internal layout (you can walk around inside your ship)
- Name the ship (appears on the map and to other players)
- Upgrade individual components over time

Ships are persistent and can be stored at orbital docks or landed on planets.

---

## 3. Space Travel

Once the ship is space-worthy:

- Launch from the main planet
- Enter orbit / open space
- Fly freely (not just fast travel)
- Land back on the main planet or any discovered planet
- Scan for new planets
- Engage in space combat or avoid it
- Dock at rare orbital stations or player-built space bases

Flying should feel weighty and exciting, not just a loading screen.

---

## 4. Infinite Procedurally Generated Planets

This is the big one.

### Generation Philosophy
- Effectively infinite planets
- Each planet has a seed and can be revisited
- Planets are generated with a combination of:
  - Biome theme
  - Size
  - Resource richness
  - Danger level
  - Special features / anomalies
  - Life forms

### Example Planet Types (not a fixed list — the generator creates variations)
- Lush forest worlds
- Ice / tundra planets
- Desert / dune worlds
- Volcanic / ash planets
- Ocean worlds (mostly water)
- Crystal / mineral planets
- Metal / gold-rich planets (entire regions of pure ore)
- Toxic / poison atmospheres
- Low-gravity or high-gravity worlds
- Ancient ruin planets
- Dinosaur / prehistoric planets
- Fungal / bioluminescent worlds
- Dead / barren rocks
- Living planets (the ground is alive)
- Pocket dimension style anomalies

### What Can Exist on Planets
- Unique trees, rocks, and harvestable nodes
- Entirely new animals and creatures (including dinosaurs and stranger things)
- New fish in alien lakes and oceans
- Rare materials that don’t exist on the main planet
- Ancient structures and mini-dungeons
- Boss-level creatures
- Environmental hazards
- Perfect spots to build temporary or permanent outposts

Players can claim small areas, build bases, set up mining operations, or just strip the planet and leave.

---

## 5. Progression & Risk

- Early ships are slow, fragile, and limited in range
- Better materials = better ships = farther and more dangerous planets
- Some planets are so rich they become legendary (players will share coordinates)
- Dying in space or on a hostile planet has consequences (ship damage, lost cargo, temporary setbacks) but should never feel completely punishing
- Players can eventually build multiple ships or a small fleet

---

## 6. Integration with Existing Systems

- **Pets** can come with you into space (some pets even give space bonuses)
- **Fishing** works on alien planets (completely new fish tables)
- **Hunting** works on new creatures
- **Building** works on other planets (outposts, mining bases, etc.)
- **Beastcaller** can morph into alien creatures once discovered
- **Materials** from other planets feed back into better ships, weapons, and tools
- **Duels** can happen on other planets or even in space

---

## 7. Long-Term Fantasy

A player should be able to:
1. Spend early game gathering and building their first ship
2. Make their first nervous jump to a nearby planet
3. Discover something incredible (a gold planet, a dinosaur world, a living ocean)
4. Return with rare materials and become a legend
5. Eventually maintain multiple outposts across different planets
6. Build a ship so advanced it becomes a mobile base

This system turns the game from “open world ARPG” into “open world ARPG + space frontier sandbox”.

---

This is now a foundational system. Everything else (classes, pets, fishing, building, etc.) feeds into it and is fed by it.
