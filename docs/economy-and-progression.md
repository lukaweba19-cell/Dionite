# Economy & How Everything Feeds Power

## Currencies
- **Gold** — primary
- **Soul Fragments** — original endgame currency
- **Fish Scales / Pearls** — fishing specific
- **Hunt Marks / Trophies** — hunting specific
- **Build Tokens** — for advanced building pieces
- **Bond Essence** — pet related

## Major Gold Sinks
- Weapon / rod / vehicle upgrades
- Special perk rerolls (very important sink)
- Pet food and bonding items
- Building materials at scale
- Boat and vehicle maintenance
- Cosmetic and collection systems

## Power Fantasy Integration
Lifestyle systems are not side content — they directly feed power:

- Better fishing → more gold → better gear
- Better pets → more luck → better drops and fish
- Hunting → materials for high-end crafting + morph unlocks
- Building → personal bases that give permanent quality-of-life and small combat bonuses
- Vehicles → faster travel = more efficient farming

This creates a beautiful web where doing "fun" things makes you stronger.
