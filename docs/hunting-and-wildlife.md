# Hunting & Wildlife

## Design Goal
Make the world feel populated with animals that are worth interacting with — not just background decoration.

## Animal Categories
- Common prey (deer, rabbits, birds)
- Dangerous predators
- Rare / legendary beasts
- Magical / elemental creatures
- Mount candidates
- Pet candidates

## Hunting Loop
1. Track (tracks, sounds, scent trails — especially strong for Beastcaller)
2. Approach / stalk
3. Engage (combat or specialized hunting tools)
4. Harvest

Successful hunts give:
- Meat / hides / bones / rare organs
- Chance at unique trophies
- Progress toward taming or morph unlocks (Beastcaller)
- Pet eggs or young animals

## Tools
- Bows and crossbows (strong synergy)
- Throwing knives / spears
- Traps
- Specialized hunting rifles (Artificer)
- Bait and lures

Animals have different behaviors: flight, fight, call for help, go into dens, etc.

Some rare animals only appear during specific weather, times, or world events.
