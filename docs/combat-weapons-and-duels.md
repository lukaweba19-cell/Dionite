# Combat, Weapons & Player Duels

## Expanded Weapon Types

Beyond the original modular gun system, the game now supports a wide variety of weapon families:

| Family | Examples | Style |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Melee Blades | Knives, daggers, swords, greatswords, katanas | Fast / heavy |
| Blunt | Maces, hammers, staves | High stagger |
| Polearms | Spears, halberds, glaives | Reach |
| Bows & Crossbows | Shortbow, longbow, compound, crossbows | Ranged physical |
| Staffs / Wands | Elemental focuses | Spellcasting |
| Firearms | Pistols, rifles, shotguns, exotic guns | Artificer focus |
| Thrown | Knives, axes, javelins, bombs | Utility |
| Exotic / Mythic | Living weapons, cursed blades, cosmic arms | Unique mechanics |

All weapons support the existing **socket system** and gain:

### Charms
Small attachable items that add focused bonuses (more modular than full enchantments).

### Special Perk Slot (Rerollable)
Every weapon of Rare+ has one **Special Perk** slot.

This perk is **highly variable** and can be rerolled (with cost).

Examples of possible rolls:
- +x10 ~ x60 Luck
- +x20 ~ x50 Carry Capacity / Backpack space
- Massive gold find
- On-kill heal
- Chance to duplicate fish or hunting drops
- Building piece limit increase
- Vehicle speed / durability
- Morph duration (Beastcaller)
- Unique on-hit effects

The special perk is intentionally chaotic and exciting — players will chase specific high rolls.

## Player vs Player — Duels

### Instant Duel System
- When one player hits another player in the open world, a **Duel to the Death** is automatically proposed/started.
- Both players get a short confirmation window (can decline if not wanting to fight).
- Once accepted (or if forced in certain zones), it becomes a full duel.

### Rules
- Loser loses **nothing** (no gold, no items, no durability loss).
- Winner receives:
  - Gold (scaled to both players' levels and recent activity)
  - Chance at bonus rewards (rare materials, fish, pet treats, temporary buffs, etc.)
- Optional: ranked duel mode with leaderboards later.

This creates fun, low-stakes PvP that encourages interaction without the toxicity of full loot PvP.

Players can also flag for open-world PvP in certain regions if they want higher risk/reward.
