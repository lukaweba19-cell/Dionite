# Fishing — Going Extremely Deep

You asked for 300+ fish. I’m treating that as a minimum and designing the system so it can support 500+ without feeling samey.

### Fish Philosophy
Every fish should have a *reason* to exist:
- Economic (sells for gold)
- Crafting (unique materials)
- Collection (looks cool)
- Mechanical (used in specific recipes or offerings)
- Story (tied to a location or event)

### Special Fish Categories I’m Adding
- **Boss Fish** — giant fish that fight back hard and drop unique trophies
- **Living Relics** — fish that are actually ancient artifacts
- **Parasite Fish** — rare fish that can infest other fish and create new variants
- **Migratory Legends** — only appear once per real-world week in specific locations
- **Player-Named Fish** — extremely rare, the first player to catch a new species can name it permanently on that server

### Rod Progression Fantasy
Rods should feel like weapons:
- Early rods are tools
- Mid rods are status symbols
- Endgame rods are almost legendary weapons that just happen to catch fish

Some rods will have personalities or talk to you (very rare).

### The Aquarium & Living Collection
Players can build personal aquariums in their bases.  
High-end aquariums give small passive bonuses based on what fish are displayed.  
Rare fish on display can attract visitors and even special NPCs.
