# Pets System

Pets are one of the highest priority fun systems.

## Core Loop
1. Find / hatch / buy / bond with a pet
2. Level and bond with it
3. Equip it for passive boosts + active ability
4. Collect many different looks and rarities

## Pet Categories
- **Companion** — general bonuses
- **Luck** — pure luck / find / gold find
- **Combat** — damage, defense, on-hit effects
- **Lifestyle** — fishing, hunting, building, movement
- **Mythic / Cosmic** — extremely rare, game-changing

## Passive Boosts (examples)
Pets can boost any combination of:
- Luck
- Gold Find
- Magic Find
- Fishing Luck & Power
- Hunting success & quality
- Experience gain
- Resource generation
- Movement speed
- Carry capacity / backpack space
- Building piece limit
- Vehicle stats

Multiple pets can be collected. Players equip **1 active pet** + unlock **passive pet slots** through progression (up to 3–5 late game).

## Bonding & Happiness
- Pets have a happiness / bond meter
- Feeding, playing, bringing them on successful hunts/fishing trips increases it
- High bond = stronger bonuses + unique active ability unlocks
- Neglect reduces bonuses

## Visuals
Every pet has distinct looks ranging from basic (common dog, cat, bird) all the way to cosmic / elemental / mythical creatures with unique particle effects and animations.

Pets should feel collectible and expressive — players will want to show them off.
