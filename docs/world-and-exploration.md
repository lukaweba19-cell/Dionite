# World & Exploration — Open World, Building, Vehicles

## Design Goal
Move from "dungeon runner with overworld hubs" to a true open-world sandbox while keeping the strong combat and progression DNA of the original design.

Players should be able to:
- Freely roam a large continuous world
- Build almost anywhere (Fortnite-style)
- Fish, hunt, and tame in every major biome
- Discover hidden lakes, ruins, and events
- Travel by foot, mount, boat, or vehicle

---

## World Structure

### Continuous Open World
- Large seamless overworld (chunk-streamed like the existing StreamingManager)
- Original 5 biomes expanded + new transitional and secret biomes
- Dungeons and boss arenas still exist but are now **embedded** in the world rather than the primary content loop

### Expanded Biome List

| Biome | Feel | Key Features |
|-------|------|--------------|
| Verdant Wilds | Lush forests, rivers | Dense fishing ponds, high animal density, easy building |
| Ashen Wastes | Volcanic, scorched | Rare fire fish, lava lakes, aggressive fauna |
| Frozen Spire | Ice & snow peaks | Frozen lakes, ice fishing, frost beasts |
| Sunken Crypts | Flooded ruins | Underwater fishing zones, drowned wildlife |
| Sky Citadel | Floating islands | Cloud fishing (yes), aerial creatures |
| **Jungle Canopy** (new) | Dense tropical | Highest fish variety, canopy walking, poison animals |
| **Misty Marshes** (new) | Foggy wetlands | Rare amphibious fish, bog vehicles |
| **Crystal Caverns** (new) | Underground | Glow fish, crystal hunting, no sunlight |
| **Shattered Isles** (new) | Archipelago | Best boats, island hopping, unique sea life |

Every biome has multiple **named ponds, lakes, rivers, and coastlines** with unique fish tables.

## Building System (Fortnite-inspired)

Players can build almost anywhere in the open world (with some protected zones around major towns and boss arenas).

### Building Modes
- **Instant Build** — hold build button → place pieces quickly (Fortnite style)
- **Precision Mode** — slower, perfect placement, free-form rotation
- **Blueprint Mode** — save and re-use structures

### Materials
- Wood (common, flammable)
- Stone
- Metal
- Crystal / Aetherium (late game, special properties)
- Organic / Living wood (from certain trees, regenerates slowly)

### Piece Types
- Walls, floors, roofs, stairs, ramps
- Foundations & pillars
- Windows, doors, gates
- Defensive pieces (spikes, traps, arrow slits)
- Decorative & functional (chests, workbenches, pet beds, fishing docks)

### Building Rules
- Soft limit on pieces per player (upgradeable via pets, paragon, and special perks)
- Structures can be claimed and shared with friends
- Enemy players can damage/destroy buildings during open-world conflict or duels
- Weather and biome events can damage unprotected builds

Building is not just cosmetic — good bases give storage, crafting speed, pet happiness, and safe fishing/hunting spots.

## Vehicles & Travel

### Land
- Mounts (horses, wolves, exotic beasts — tied into pet/hunting system)
- Carts / Wagons (storage focused)
- All-terrain vehicles (late game, rare schematics)

### Water
- Rafts → Rowboats → Sailboats → Steamships → Magical skiffs
- Different boats have different speed, cargo, fishing bonuses, and durability

### Air (late game)
- Gliders
- Hot air balloons
- Limited flying mounts

Vehicles can be upgraded with sockets, charms, and special perks just like weapons.

---

## Dynamic World Events (expanded)
- Blood Moon / Helltide style events
- Massive fish migrations
- Animal stampedes
- Treasure storms
- Wandering merchants with unique vehicles and rods
- Player-triggered invasions

The world should feel alive and worth exploring even when not actively grinding dungeons.
